# Travel policy

Reimbursement limits: economy airfare for flights under six hours; premium economy above six hours. Hotel reimbursement is capped at 180 USD per night in standard cities and 260 USD per night in high-cost cities.

Approval steps: all international travel requires manager approval at least 14 days before departure, and finance approval for trips above 3,000 USD.

Exceptions: exceptions to the travel policy require written approval from a director and must be attached to the expense report.
